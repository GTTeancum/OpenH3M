images of Beaver Creek, Hemorrhage or other canyon maps.

No remote image was downloaded to bypass display restrictions. Search snippets
and page captions establish reference identity, not our own visual inspection.
Inspect the images themselves alongside actual MCC captures before judging
materials, lighting, geometry or sky. Publication screenshots have unknown
display/gamma settings and cannot supply a calibrated brightness target.

The user-approved use of H3 materials/effects remains binding. If a required
visual feature has no stock alternative, obtain a specific exception instead
of quietly authoring a custom replacement. Still verify flicker while moving,
collision/traversal, audio and gameplay in the actual MCC test; static reference
images cannot establish those behaviors.
